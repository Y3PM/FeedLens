## 简体中文

### 标题

觅读订阅 · FeedLens - RSS 订阅源发现与预览

### 简短说明

发现网页上的 RSS、Atom 订阅源，预览文章与 OPML 订阅列表，复制订阅链接或跳转常用 RSS 阅读器完成订阅。

### 详细介绍

浏览博客、新闻或其他网站时，觅读订阅帮你找到网站提供的订阅源，先看看内容，再决定是否订阅。在发现订阅源的网页上，点击浮动 RSS 按钮或扩展图标，即可打开预览。

- 订阅源预览：将 RSS、Atom 等 XML 订阅源展示为文章列表与内容预览，保留原文链接。
- OPML 列表预览：按分组查看订阅源卡片，打开单个订阅源或复制链接。
- 连接阅读器：复制订阅地址，或通过 Subscribe 菜单将链接交给外部阅读器；也可设置自定义阅读器 URL 模板。订阅在目标阅读器中完成。
- 查看源文件：随时打开原始 XML 或 OPML，方便核对内容。
- 键盘浏览：使用上下方向键或 J/K 切换文章。

本地 OPML 文件需先在扩展设置中开启“允许访问文件网址”。预览界面目前使用英文。

觅得好文，读得自在。

## English

### Title

FeedLens - RSS Feed Finder & Preview

### Summary

Discover RSS and Atom feeds on websites, preview feeds and OPML lists, and subscribe with your favorite RSS reader.

### Detailed description

Find a website's feed while browsing blogs, news, and other sites, then explore its content before subscribing. When FeedLens discovers a feed, use the floating RSS button or extension icon to open a preview.

- Preview RSS, Atom, and other supported XML feeds as an article list with a content preview and links to the original posts.
- Explore OPML subscription lists as grouped feed cards. Open individual feeds or copy their links.
- Connect to your reader by copying a feed URL or choosing an external service from the Subscribe menu. Custom reader URL templates are also supported. Subscriptions are completed in the destination reader.
- Open the original XML or OPML whenever you need to inspect the source.
- Browse entries with the arrow keys or J/K.

To preview local OPML files, enable “Allow access to file URLs” in the extension settings. The preview interface is currently in English.

Feed your curiosity.
